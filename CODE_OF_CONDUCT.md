# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people of every background and
experience level. Contributors, maintainers, testers, and users are expected to
act with respect, patience, honesty, and care for other people's data.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Respect differing experience, identity, viewpoint, and communication style.
- Credit other people's work and preserve licenses.
- Disclose testing limits and data-loss risks clearly.
- Remove private information from logs, screenshots, issues, and pull requests.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and review.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information or credentials.
- Knowingly encouraging unsafe actions that can destroy or expose user data.
- Misrepresenting test results, provenance, authorship, or licensing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, reject pull
requests, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner using the contact information
on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/MVMC4). If no private
contact method is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact
without including sensitive details.

Reports will be considered in good faith and handled as privately as the
available channel permits. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is
not acceptable.
